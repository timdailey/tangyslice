The leads aren't
coming back.
Not because your campaigns got worse.

Not because your team stopped trying. Not because you picked the wrong tool or hired the wrong agency or failed to optimize the right sequence at the right time.

They're not coming back because the world they lived in doesn't exist anymore.

The indictment

For twenty years, the Agency/SaaS Industrial Complex sold you a system. Fill the funnel. Score the leads. Automate the nurture. Renew the retainer. Upgrade the plan. They built an interlocking machine of tools and services, each one justifying the other, each one requiring the next, all of it running on the assumption that buyers would keep behaving the way buyers always had.

They don't.

Three things the complex got wrong

01

The system was built for a buyer journey AI just ended.

02

The lead apocalypse wasn't a slump. It was a structural collapse.

03

The tools were never built for your business.

"Here's what they won't tell you: the tools were never built for your business. The strategies were never designed for your market. The retainers were priced for enterprises and sold to companies a tenth of their size. You were paying for a system built to perpetuate itself — not to grow you."

What we built instead

We built Tangyslice because we've been inside the machine. We know how it works. We know what it costs. And we know it was already failing before AI finished it off.

We believe the companies that survive the next decade won't be the ones who optimized the old system hardest. They'll be the ones who abandoned it earliest and built something new in its place.

—

Custom AI workflows built around how your business actually operates.

—

A fractional CMO who knows the new buyer journey and doesn't pretend the old one still functions.

—

Tools you own, built for your specific market, that work while you sleep — without feeding the complex a monthly fee to keep the lights on.

Who this is for

We work with lean teams at small tech companies. The ones making real decisions without enterprise budgets or armies of specialists. The ones who've sat across the table from an agency and felt the slow creep of being handled. The ones who suspected the MQL dashboard was telling a story that never matched what was actually happening in their market.

You were right.

The new buyer doesn't want to be nurtured. They want to be understood. They want to find you when they're already looking — in AI search results, in peer communities, in tools that demonstrate your thinking before you ever get on a call. They want proof that you understand their problem specifically, not a personalized subject line on a generic email.

That's what we build.

The funnel is dead.

The complex is burning.

Build something better.
