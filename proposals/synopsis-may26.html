About Synopsis

The foundation for
AI-powered business.

Synopsis is an AI data infrastructure platform built for executives who know they need to leverage AI — but don't know where to start. It connects every system, cleans and unifies data automatically, resolves duplicate records, and delivers insights through natural language queries.

The platform compresses what traditionally takes months of data engineering work into days — no data team required. Self-service analytics, live dashboards, and AI-generated insights are alive and generating actionable answers within a week of setup.

Key capabilities include pre-built connectors for 200+ applications, automatic data modeling, entity resolution, natural language querying, and live dashboards that update automatically.

The Opportunity

Strong product.
Market infrastructure to build.

Synopsis has a compelling product story and strong early traction. The opportunity ahead is to build the systems that take it beyond direct outreach — the positioning, content, pipeline, and outbound motion that the product has outrun.

01
Thin top-of-funnel
Minimal content marketing, SEO/GEO, or thought leadership to generate inbound demand beyond direct sales outreach. The site can only rank for branded terms and a handful of head terms. Buyers doing independent research find nothing.
02
Underleveraged differentiation
The "no data team required, days not months" story is the most compelling thing Synopsis can say. It is not yet translated into the language buyers use, in the places they look, with the proof points that make it credible at scale.
03
No demand generation motion
No content flywheel, no nurture infrastructure, and no pipeline model. Every new conversation starts from zero. The mid-market buyer doing independent research encounters nothing.
04
Low brand awareness beyond direct network
Limited social presence and no content marketing signals that Synopsis hasn't scaled beyond its immediate network. The product is ahead of the market infrastructure that makes it discoverable and credible to new buyers.

What Success Looks Like

Four outcomes.
Ninety days of foundation.

This engagement is the foundation of a longer trajectory. The three months we work together are designed to generate returns that extend well beyond the contract period.

A single, aligned positioning statement and ICP that the entire team — sales, product, founders — is working from. A refreshed deck and enablement resources that reduce friction in every deal conversation. No more translating the product in the room.

Updated copy and CTAs that speak to buyer problems, not technology features. Specifically tuned to Synopsis's "no data team required" narrative. The website earns the demo request rather than asking for it.

How We Get There

Structured work.
Three phases.

Work moves in three sequential phases. Each builds directly on the one before it — we do not activate anything until the strategy and assets are solid. A sprint-based project management cadence with bi-weekly planning runs throughout all three phases.

Phase 1 — Foundation · Days 1–30 · Strategy, positioning, and research
—Foundational strategy & positioning — market positioning statement and messaging hierarchy, specifically addressing the "no data team required, days not months" differentiation
—ICP and persona development — ideal customer profile with personas mapped to Synopsis's "executives who know they need AI but don't know where to start" buyer
—Competitive landscape — differentiation framework mapping Synopsis against data engineering consultancies, BI platforms, and emerging AI data tools
—Brand identity alignment — voice guidelines and brand identity alignment to support consistent execution across all channels
—Audit of programs, tech stack, and website — full review of CRM, martech stack, existing analytics, website content, and automation workflows
—Gap analysis — structured output to guide plan-building across phases 2 and 3
Phase 2 — Build · Days 31–60 · Content, enablement, and SEO/GEO
—Refreshed sales deck and core enablement resources — reoriented around buyer problems, not platform features
—Website copy and CTA recommendations — spec all improvements for Synopsis's team to implement; copy that earns the demo request
—Case study development — interview-based proof asset, anonymized where necessary
—Content strategy mapped to customer journey — editorial approach tied to buyer stages and keyword targets
—SEO/GEO strategy and entity building — keyword and content gap analysis, targeting the AI-search layer where Synopsis's buyers increasingly discover solutions
—GTM plan, funnel model, and lead scoring — go-to-market plan with prioritized segment sequencing, nurture sequence build (1–2 sequences), and lead scoring model
Phase 3 — Activate · Days 61–90 · Outbound, distribution, and launch
—8 SEO/GEO blog posts — researched, written, and LinkedIn-adapted; targeting high-intent data infrastructure queries with entity structure built in
—Glossary pages — AI-drafted, edited, and schema-formatted; targeting definitional queries in data infrastructure and AI readiness where Synopsis can own the cited answer
—GEO-optimized FAQ pages — structured Q&A content with FAQPage schema, directly targeted at AI engine citation
—Social media strategy and 4-week content calendar template — repeatable framework for Synopsis's team to continue executing
—Founder-led LinkedIn ghostwriting — 4 posts, AI-assisted thought leadership in the founder's voice and ready to publish
—PR/AR outreach — 5–8 targeted pitches to tech and data infrastructure media
—Events strategy framework — event selection criteria and participation model for conferences and webinars
—Metrics framework, reporting dashboards, and H2 marketing plan — fully documented plan with sprint cadence ready to execute or hand off at day 90
Entity & GEO Optimization — woven throughout all three phases

AI engines must clearly understand who Synopsis is before any content strategy compounds. We audit and align Synopsis's entity signals across LinkedIn, Crunchbase, directory listings, schema markup, and founder bio pages — ensuring every surface tells a consistent, citable story. This work runs in parallel from day one, not as a Phase 3 afterthought.

Scope

What's in.
What's out.

To keep the engagement focused and executable at 20 hours per week, the following are explicitly out of scope. Each is a deliberate choice, not a gap.

Out of scope
Paid advertising
Requires dedicated budget, creative, and ongoing management that the current hour model doesn't support well. Recommended for H2 once positioning is proven and channels have signal.
In scope instead
Organic and earned channels
SEO/GEO content, PR/AR outreach, social strategy, and founder LinkedIn — all channels that compound over time and don't require ongoing ad spend to maintain.
Out of scope
Website development or redesign
Copy and CTA recommendations are in scope; implementation is a client-side or separate engagement. Tangyslice specs and writes; Synopsis's team ships.
In scope instead
Website copy and CTA spec
Every page that needs new copy gets a full written spec with CTA recommendations — ready to implement without additional briefing.
Out of scope
Full events program production
Event planning and logistics are out of scope. Strategy framework and event selection criteria are in — enough to act on without committing to production overhead.
In scope instead
Events strategy framework
A prioritized list of target events, participation criteria, and a repeatable format for how Synopsis should show up — conference, webinar, or panel.
Out of scope
Ongoing social media management
Day-to-day posting and community management are not included. The engagement produces the strategy and calendar template; execution stays with Synopsis's team.
In scope instead
Social strategy and calendar template
A documented social media strategy and 4-week content calendar template Synopsis's team can execute and repeat without additional briefing.
