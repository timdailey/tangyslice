Logo usage rules

Always use the provided logo files. Never recreate the mark, stretch the logo, change the colors, or add effects. Minimum display size: 120px wide.

On dark backgrounds, apply filter: brightness(0) invert(1) opacity(0.75) in CSS to invert the logo for legibility.

Gold is dark-background only

Gold (#E8D080) disappears on the cream background — it's too close in value. Reserve it exclusively for dark (#1A0A06) sections: eyebrow labels, accents, footer column headers. Never use it on cream or white.

Red is the sole action color

Every interactive element — CTA buttons, text links, pull quote rules, accent headlines — uses red. It signals interaction and importance. Use it with intention, not decoration. Don't use red for body copy or general emphasis.

09 — Brand voice & tone

The Tangyslice voice is direct, principled, and wry. It sounds like someone who has been inside the machine, understands exactly how it works, and decided to build something better. It never hedges. It never oversells. It says the uncomfortable thing plainly.

Write like this

The leads aren't coming back. Not because your campaigns got worse — because the buyer journey AI ended.
We don't take on every engagement. Not because we're precious — because the work only holds up when there's a genuine fit.
No proposal theater. No 40-page decks. A plain document that says what we'd do, how long it would take, and what it would cost.
You were right. The new buyer doesn't want to be nurtured. They want to be understood.

Not like this

We leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to optimize your marketing ROI across all channels.
Our team of experts delivers best-in-class fractional CMO services tailored to your unique needs.
We're passionate about helping brands unlock their full potential in the digital landscape.
Schedule a free discovery call to explore how we can accelerate your growth journey.

Declarative

Make statements. Don't qualify everything. "The funnel is dead." Not "The funnel may no longer be as effective as it once was."

Specific

Name the enemy. Name the buyer. Name the problem. "Lean SMB tech teams" is more credible than "growing companies."

Honest

Say who you don't work with. Say what you actually do. Acknowledge the limits. Trust is built by saying the thing other firms won't.
